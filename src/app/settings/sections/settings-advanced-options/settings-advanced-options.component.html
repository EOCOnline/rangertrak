Mission backup

Backs up this device’s entire mission — settings, rangers and field reports — to a single file, so it survives clearing browser storage or a move to another device.

The exported file includes the ranger roster — legal names, personal phone numbers and call signs — and is not encrypted. Store it accordingly and delete it when the mission is over.

warning Danger zone

Each of these replaces or discards data already on this device, and cannot be undone. Export your mission first if you need to keep it.

The sample mission is a roster of 12 units and about 30 field reports spread across Vashon Island over the last several hours — useful for demonstrating RangerTrak, or for seeing how a screen looks with realistic data on a brand-new install.
