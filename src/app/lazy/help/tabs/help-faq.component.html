Frequently asked

Do I need an account, a login, or an API key?

No. Everything RangerTrak does to enter, convert, map, export and back up reports works with no account and no key. The one exception is optional: you may supply your own Google key on the Mission page to use Google for address lookup instead of the free OpenStreetMap default. Nothing requires it.

Will it work with no Internet?

Yes, if it has been loaded on this device at least once, and you saved map coverage for your area beforehand. A device that has never opened RangerTrak needs a connection to get started.

Do I have to press Save?

It depends where you are, which is worth knowing before you lose work. On the Mission page, press Save. On the Rangers page, edits typed into the grid need Save edits under Advanced — though importing, adding and deleting save themselves. On the Radio Log page, corrections save as soon as you leave the cell; there is no button to remember.

A coordinate format disappeared from the entry screen.

It has been switched off for this mission, under Mission → Location Defaults. The Show all coordinate systems toggle on the entry screen brings them back for this session.

What happened to What3Words?

It's shelved pending a rewrite against a newer version of their service, so it isn't offered anywhere in the app right now. Plus Codes do the same job and are calculated on your device, so they keep working with no connection.

What do the marker shapes and the lines mean?

Each ranger gets their own marker shape and colour, from their call sign, so they look the same everywhere. Lines join one ranger's reports oldest to newest, and the label shows how long ago the most recent one came in.

The Radio Log page looks different on my phone.

On a phone it becomes a read-only list of cards, because the full table cannot be used accurately at that width. Corrections need a wider screen.

How do I get the roster onto another device?

Export roster on the Rangers page, then Import roster on the other device. That moves only the roster. To move a whole mission, use Export Mission on the Mission page instead.

A new version appeared. Is it safe to reload mid-mission?

Yes. Reports are already saved on the device, not held in the page, so reloading takes a few seconds and loses nothing.

Can other people see my data?

Only if they have this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The exception is anything you send through the feedback form, which becomes a public issue — so keep mission data out of it.

Can several people watch the same log at once?

Not yet. Each device holds its own copy, and sharing means exporting. Serving the log to nearby devices over the command post's own network is planned.
