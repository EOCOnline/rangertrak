Starting a device over

Delete the reports on the Radio Log page and the roster on the Rangers page, then reset settings under Mission → Advanced Options. Export anything you need first — none of it can be undone.

Load Sample Mission, Import Mission and Reset mission to defaults all replace what is on this device. Export first if you need to keep it. See Your data for what a mission's data actually is and where it lives.

What to keep, what to clear

After the mission Do this
Keep a record of what happened Export Mission (Mission → Advanced Options) before clearing anything — settings, roster and reports in one file
Clear reports for the next mission Radio Log → Delete all field reports
Reuse the same roster next time Leave the roster alone — it is not tied to a single mission and does not need to be re-imported
Start completely fresh Mission → Advanced Options → Reset mission to defaults, plus clearing the roster and reports above

The roster holds participant personal data. If this device is being handed off, reused for another purpose, or is not yours, clear the roster too — see Your data for why it is confidential.
