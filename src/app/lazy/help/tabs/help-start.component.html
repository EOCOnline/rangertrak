The first five minutes on a new device

  1. Open Mission and set the mission name and operational period.
  2. Open Rangers and load your roster.
  3. Go back to Field Entry (aka the home page, click the RangerTrak name to go there). That is where you will spend the incident.

The coloured dot in the page header tracks those steps. Hover it for what is still missing; click it to jump to Mission.

Do this before you need it. RangerTrak works with no Internet — but only once it has loaded at least once on this device. A device that has never opened RangerTrak and has no connection gets nothing. Install it, and open the map for your area, while you still have a signal.
