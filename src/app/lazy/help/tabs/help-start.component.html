What RangerTrak is

A log of who is where, when, and in what condition, for teams reachable only by voice radio. Field teams radio in; you transcribe; RangerTrak maps it and keeps the record.

It runs entirely in this browser, on this device. No server, no account, no login. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

The first five minutes on a new device

  1. Open Mission and set the mission name and operational period.
  2. Open Rangers and load your roster.
  3. Go back to Field Entry. That is where you will spend the incident.

The coloured dot in the page header tracks those steps. Hover it for what is still missing; click it to jump to Mission.

Do this before you need it. RangerTrak works with no Internet — but only once it has loaded at least once on this device. A device that has never opened RangerTrak and has no connection gets nothing. Install it, and open the map for your area, while you still have a signal.

Two phases, even when one person does both

Setup is infrequent and considered — Mission and Rangers, before or between operational periods. Entry is repetitive and time-critical — the entry screen, while reports come in. The app is built around the second one being fast.
