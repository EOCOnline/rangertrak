Build a Mission Zip

Packages THIS device's current roster, locations, ranger photos, and mission settings into one downloadable .zip. Deliberately does not include field reports - a Mission Zip is a pre-mission template, not a mid-mission backup (use Mission Setup's own "Export mission" for that).

Confidential: a Mission Zip contains real names, phone numbers and photographs of volunteers. Do not email it, do not put it in source control, and wipe the drive it's on after the mission.
Load a Mission Zip

Replaces this device's roster, locations, mission settings, and EVERY ranger photo (not just the ones this zip has a replacement for) with the contents of a Mission Zip someone handed you. Field reports already on this device are never touched - a Mission Zip has none of its own to bring in.
